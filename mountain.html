The Andes Mountains (South America)

Cruise Attraction

Passengers can take the mountain railways to explore ancient ruins like Machu Picchu, or hike past snow-capped peaks, high-altitude salt flats, and active volcanoes.

How It Was Created

The Andes Mountains are where the South American and Nazca tectonic plates meet at a convergent plate boundary. Where the two plates meet, the land is pushed up, forming a mountain range.

Convergent Boundary Subduction Zone Convection

The Andes Mountains run across the west coast of South America.
